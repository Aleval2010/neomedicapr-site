About NeoMedica

Puerto Rico has the talent. America needs the care.

NeoMedica was founded on a simple observation: Puerto Rico produces exceptional, bilingual, U.S.-licensed nurses — and the U.S. mainland has a deep and growing shortage of clinical capacity for Remote Patient Monitoring. We connect the two.

Our mission

Keep nursing talent on the island — and put it to work

For years, Puerto Rico's best nurses have faced a hard choice: leave the island for mainland opportunity, or stay and accept fewer options. That migration drains the local healthcare system and fractures families.

NeoMedica changes the equation. By bringing U.S. provider work to nurses where they live, we keep talent rooted in Puerto Rico while opening careers that previously required relocation — and we give U.S. providers a credentialed, compliant, bilingual workforce in the process.
